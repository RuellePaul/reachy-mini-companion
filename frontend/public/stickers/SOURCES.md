# Stickers — origin

(`src/Sticker.tsx`). Copyright their authors. SVGs optimised with `svgo 3.3.2` (`--multipass`, float precision 2,
viewBox kept); PNGs cropped to their sticker and saved as WebP (320 px, quality 88).
